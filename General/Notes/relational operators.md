<, >, <=, >=, ==, != are generally called **relational operators** or **comparison operators**

They compare two values and usually produce a Boolean result (true or false).

| Operator | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `<` | Less than | `3 < 5` → `true` |
| `>` | Greater than | `5 > 3` → `true` |
| `<=` | Less than or equal to | `3 <= 3` → `true` |
| `>=` | Greater than or equal to | `5 >= 5` → `true` |
| `==` | Equal to (loose equality) | `5 == "5"` → `true` |
| `!=` | Not equal to (loose inequality) | `5 != "3"` → `true` |
| `===` | Strictly equal to | `5 === "5"` → `false` |
| `!==` | Strictly not equal to | `5 !== "5"` → `true` |
